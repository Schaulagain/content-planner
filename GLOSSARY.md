# Content planner

A personal tool for planning, posting and learning from short nature videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

## Planning

**Idea**:
Something worth filming: a place, a moment, and the thought the caption might carry. Every video starts as an idea.
_Avoid_: draft, concept

**Stage**:
Where an idea is on its way to being posted: idea, filmed, edited, posted or reviewed.
_Avoid_: status, column

**Series**:
A named run of related videos, numbered in the order they're posted, like "Places that make you slow down #3".
_Avoid_: playlist, category

**Caption**:
The short line shown on the video and in its description. A good one says something about the place.
_Avoid_: hook text, title

## Posting

**Post**:
An idea once it has gone up. It records when it was posted and the caption actually used. One post can go to several platforms.
_Avoid_: upload, video (when meaning the record)

**Platform**:
Where a post goes up: TikTok, Instagram or YouTube.
_Avoid_: channel, network

**Stats**:
The numbers a platform shows for one post, written down about 48 hours after posting: views, likes, comments, shares, saves and average watch time.
_Avoid_: analytics, metrics

## Learning

**Review**:
The weekly look back, done on Sunday: this week's posts and their stats, and one line about what was learned.
_Avoid_: retro, report

**Learning**:
The one line a review leaves behind, like "Captions with a thought get shared more."
_Avoid_: insight, takeaway
